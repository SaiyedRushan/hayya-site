Frequently asked questions

My alarms aren't going off on time.

Hayya relies on your phone's exact-alarm and notification permissions. Make sure notifications are enabled for Hayya, that exact alarms are allowed, and that battery optimization is turned off for the app so the system doesn't delay it. On some devices you may need to allow Hayya to run in the background.

The full-screen "call" alarm doesn't appear over my lock screen.

Please grant Hayya permission to display over other apps and to show full-screen notifications. These let the alarm surface over the lock screen.

My prayer times look wrong.

Check that your location is correct and pick the calculation method and madhab used in your region in Settings. You can also adjust the Hijri date by a day or two if it differs from your local sighting.

Is my data private?

Yes. Hayya keeps everything on your device — no account, no servers, no tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my data?

Uninstalling Hayya removes all of its data from your device.
